When no one has exactly what you need

Two businesses rarely want each other's things. DASKIES looks across all its members and links three or four of them into one swap.

  • Business A has machinery and needs property.
  • Business B has property and needs raw materials.
  • Business C has raw materials and needs machinery.

No two of them match, but together they do. Each business agrees its own values and documents, and any difference is settled in cash.

Questions businesses ask us

Can't find your answer? WhatsApp us or write to info@daskies.com.

How do I list something?

Join free, complete your business profile, then choose List an asset. Add photos, the details and what you'd take in exchange. Our team checks each listing before it goes live.

Can I deal on my own, or does DASKIES help?

Both. You can talk to other members and agree terms in a deal room yourself. When you want help, our team finds the other party, values swaps and handles the paperwork.

Who can see my contact details?

Only you and the DASKIES team. Other members see your business name, city and verification level, and reach you through DASKIES messages until you choose to share your number.

How does a barter deal settle?

Both sides agree a value for what they give. If the values differ, the gap is paid in cash. When two businesses can't swap directly, DASKIES links three or four of them into one chain.

Can a foreign company lease a factory in India?

Usually yes, within India's FEMA and FDI rules. The structure depends on your country, sector and how long you need the capacity, so take advice before you sign. Our Global Buyer Desk can help.

Can DASKIES handle the legal paperwork?

Yes, if you need it. Advocates and chartered accountants on the DASKIES panel prepare and check lease deeds, sale and swap agreements, title documents, stamp duty and GST. The fee is quoted per deal, before any work starts.

What does DASKIES cost?

Joining is free. Full membership is ₹15,000 + GST for 90 days, and a success fee applies only when a deal contract is signed. Every payment comes with a GST invoice.
