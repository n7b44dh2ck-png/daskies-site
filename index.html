Questions businesses ask us

Can't find your answer? Use the chat, WhatsApp us, or write to info@daskies.com.

How do I list something?

Register free on this page. We confirm your membership by email within 48 hours. Then you get a private members link to send full details: 3 photos, a description and a valuation report. We review it before it goes live.

Is DASKIES a broker or a marketplace?

Both. Anyone can browse, like a marketplace. When you want help, our team finds the other party, values barter swaps and handles paperwork until the deal is done.

How are listings checked?

Every listing needs three photos, a description and a valuation report. Our team checks it before it goes live, and each member profile shows its verification level. We don't guarantee any listing, so always do your own due diligence.

Who can see my contact details?

Only you and the DASKIES team. Other members see your business name, city and verification level, and reach you through DASKIES messages. You choose when to share your number.

How does a barter deal settle?

Both sides agree a value for what they give. If the values differ, the gap is paid in cash. When two businesses can't swap directly, we look for a third or fourth party to complete the chain.

Can I donate technology or education material?

Yes. Under Technology & education, you can donate computers, machines, software, books or training to schools, colleges and skill centres that need them. You can also sell or license technology.

How does buying at cost through a buyer group work?

You register what you want, where and your budget. Once enough buyers want the same thing in the same area, we acquire land there and build turnkey for a fixed charge agreed before work starts.

Can a foreign company lease a factory in India?

Usually yes, within India's FEMA and FDI rules. The structure depends on your country, sector and how long you need the capacity. We introduce you to Indian lawyers, chartered accountants and customs brokers before you commit.

What does DASKIES cost?

Joining is free. Full membership is ₹15,000 + GST for 90 days, and a success fee applies only when a deal contract is signed; see Pricing and the Terms. Featured listings, verification, the deal manager and the Global Buyer Desk are paid services, priced on request according to deal size.
