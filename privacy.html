Privacy Policy

Version of 16 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account and business details: your name, mobile number, email, business name, what the business does, registered address, city, state, PIN or postal code, country and, if you give it, GSTIN.
  • What you post and send: listings, photos, documents you upload (for example GST, PAN, ownership or valuation reports), messages, offers and deal records.
  • Payments: fee and membership payment records. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by the licensed payment provider; DASKIES does not see or store them.
  • Technical: the sign-in session kept in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising trackers.

Who can see what

  • Everyone: your business name, city and verification level, and your approved listings and their photos.
  • Only you and the DASKIES team: your name, mobile, email, registered address, PIN, GSTIN, documents and fees.
  • Members you deal with: the messages, offers and deal records you share with them.

The DASKIES team uses private details to verify businesses, review listings, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and issue invoices. We do not sell your information.

Where it is kept

The site is hosted by Netlify and the database and files by Supabase, under access rules that enforce the visibility above. Payments, when switched on, are processed by a licensed Indian payment provider.

Your choices

You can see and correct your details in your dashboard at any time. To get a copy of your information, or to close your account and delete it, contact us at info@daskies.com or through the Contact section or WhatsApp. We keep invoices and deal records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
