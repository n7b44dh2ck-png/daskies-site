Refunds and cancellations

Version of 16 November 2026.

Membership

  • Full membership runs for 90 days from payment. It does not renew unless you choose to renew.
  • You can stop using a plan at any time; it stays active until the end of the 90 days. Unused days are not refunded.
  • If DASKIES cannot provide the membership you paid for, we refund the unused part.

Success fees

  • If a deal is cancelled before your fee is paid, the fee is cancelled and nothing is owed.
  • If you paid your fee and the deal then does not complete through no fault of yours (for example, the other party does not deliver), tell us within 30 days and we refund the fee.
  • Fees for deals that complete are not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days to the account or card used to pay. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Delivery of goods

DASKIES does not sell, ship or deliver goods. Delivery, transport and inspection are arranged between the parties to each deal, as agreed in their terms.

Contact

To ask for a refund or cancel, use the Contact section or WhatsApp, and include the deal or payment reference.
