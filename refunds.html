Refunds and cancellations

Version of 16 November 2026.

Membership

  • Full membership runs for 90 days from payment and does not renew unless you choose to renew.
  • Membership fees are non-refundable.

Success fees

  • The success fee is payable when the deal contract is signed and is non-refundable.
  • If a deal is cancelled before the contract is signed, no fee is due.

Payments made in error

If you are charged twice or by mistake, we refund the extra amount within 7 working days to the account or card used to pay.

Delivery of goods

DASKIES does not sell, ship or deliver goods. Delivery, transport and inspection are arranged between the parties to each deal, as agreed in their terms.

Contact

To ask for a refund or cancel, use the Contact section or WhatsApp, and include the deal or payment reference.
