About DASKIES

Every business has something to offer and something it needs. DASKIES connects the two: the factories, landowners, traders and warehouse owners with idle machines, space, capacity and stock, and the businesses that need them, in India and across the world.

Why we built DASKIES

Across India, valuable capacity sits unused every day: machines standing between orders, production lines running one shift instead of three, sheds and godowns half empty, cold rooms waiting for the next season, surplus stock tying up working capital. At the same time, other businesses wait months for the same machines, borrow heavily to build space that already exists nearby, or turn away orders for lack of capacity.

The two sides rarely find each other. Classified sites and directories list everything and check nothing, and deals still move through phone calls, middlemen and trust built over years. DASKIES was built to change that: one network where serious businesses can see what is available, know who they are dealing with, and take a deal from first enquiry to signed contract in one place.

And because many businesses are rich in assets but short of cash, DASKIES is built for exchange as well as sale. A business that already owns something another business needs shouldn't have to find the full price in cash to get what it needs in return.

What you can do on DASKIES

  • Lease out what sits idle: factory sheds, spare production shifts, machines, land, storage and cold rooms, for a monthly income instead of a fixed cost.
  • Sell or buy: used machinery, complete production lines, commodities in bulk, property and technology.
  • Barter: swap what you have for what you need, value for value, with any difference settled in cash. When no direct match exists, DASKIES finds three- and four-business swap chains.
  • Buy together: businesses with the same need form buyer groups to own shops, showrooms, offices and units at cost.
  • Share and donate: pass on technology, equipment and learning material to businesses and institutions that can use them.

How a swap works

Barter on DASKIES is between businesses, value for value. Three examples show the idea:

A direct swap

Company A owns machinery worth ₹1 crore and needs warehouse space. Company B owns a warehouse worth ₹1 crore and needs machinery. Instead of each raising ₹1 crore, they exchange.

A swap plus cash

A manufacturer has a machine worth ₹50 lakh and wants one worth ₹65 lakh. They trade in their machine and pay the ₹15 lakh difference.

A three-business chain

A has machinery and wants property. B has property and wants raw materials. C has raw materials and wants machinery. No two of them match, but A's machinery goes to C, C's materials go to B, and B's property goes to A.

Every swap needs the agreement of all parties, a fair valuation, proper documents and the right tax treatment, including GST. DASKIES finds the match and keeps the record; the businesses agree the terms and sign the contract.

Six categories, one network

Everything on DASKIES sits in one of six categories, the same six you see when you search or post a listing. Every listing, in any category, gives the same basics: what it is, where it is, the price or rate, photos, the ways the owner will deal, and what they would take in exchange. Each category then asks for the details that matter for that kind of asset, so listings in it can be compared side by side.

Built on trust

A network is only as good as the people in it, so DASKIES is a network of known businesses, not anonymous ads.

  • Complete business profiles: every member gives their name, business, registered address and contact details before they can post, enquire or deal.
  • Reviewed listings: our team checks each listing for completeness before it goes live: photos, description and valuation.
  • Verification levels: each profile shows which documents DASKIES has reviewed, from confirmed contact details to business KYC and asset documents, so you can judge a counterparty at a glance.
  • Private until you choose: contact details stay hidden until both sides decide to share them. Members can report a listing or a member at any time.

From first enquiry to signed contract

  1. Join free and complete your business profile.
  2. List what you have or post what you need. DASKIES matches offers and needs, including multi-party swaps.
  3. Talk inside DASKIES: enquire and message without sharing your number.
  4. Open a deal room: make offers and counteroffers, approve terms and keep a clear record of every step.
  5. Sign the contract between the parties, using DASKIES agreement templates if you wish.
  6. Settle directly: payment, delivery and inspection are arranged between the parties as agreed.

For buyers outside India

India's manufacturers are a strong source of capacity and goods, but hard to find and vet from abroad. DASKIES shows prices in US dollars to overseas visitors, our main page is available in English, Hindi, French, German and Spanish, and our Global Buyer Desk can shortlist factories, arrange video walkthroughs, site visits, samples and inspection.

Services when you want more help

Members can add business verification, featured listings, valuation support through registered valuers, or a DASKIES deal manager who finds the other party, values swaps, negotiates and handles paperwork until the deal is done.

What we stand for

  • Clarity: joining is free, and our charges are set out on the Pricing page.
  • Neutrality: DASKIES is not a party to members' deals and does not hold or move members' money. Each deal is agreed and paid between the businesses involved.
  • Honesty: we review listings and documents, but we don't guarantee any listing, counterparty or outcome. Always do your own checks before you deal.
