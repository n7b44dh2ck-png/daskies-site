About DASKIES

DASKIES is a business network for India's industrial economy. Factories, traders, landowners and overseas buyers use it to lease, buy, sell or barter what they have and find what they need.

What members do here

  • Earn from what sits idle: spare machines, production shifts, factory sheds, storage and land.
  • Find what they need: capacity, equipment, commodities and space, in India and from abroad.
  • Trade without cash when it suits them: straight swaps and swaps between three or four businesses, with any difference settled in cash.
  • Buy together: businesses with the same need pool demand in buyer groups.

How it works

Every member gives full business details, so you know who you're dealing with. Our team reviews each listing for completeness before it goes live, and each business profile shows its verification level. Members enquire and message inside DASKIES, then agree terms in a deal room and sign a contract between themselves.

DASKIES is not a party to members' deals. We don't hold or move members' money, and we don't guarantee any listing, counterparty or outcome. Payment, delivery and inspection are arranged between the parties.

What it costs

Joining is free. Full membership and the success fee on signed deals are explained on the Pricing page.

Where we are

DASKIES is based in Gujarat, India, and works with businesses across India and overseas. To get in touch, see Contact us.
