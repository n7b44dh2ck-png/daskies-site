Own a shop, showroom, office or home at cost

People who want the same kind of property in the same area join one buyer group. We buy land in the area the group chooses and build it ready-to-use (turnkey) for a fixed charge agreed at the start. You pay for land and construction, not a builder's profit. Landowners can also join as partners.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you want (shop, showroom, office, flat or villa), the area you prefer and your budget.
  2. We group you with buyers who want the same thing in the same area.
  3. Our team finds and buys land there for the group.
  4. We build it ready-to-use for a fixed charge agreed before work starts.
  5. You get your unit with clear ownership papers.

Who it's for

  • Retailers and showroom owners
  • Doctors, CAs and other professionals who need offices
  • Families buying a home
  • Landowners and developers looking for JV partners

What you gain

  • No builder profit: you pay land, construction and a fixed fee
  • You choose the location and design
  • Construction charge fixed before work starts
