How it works

  1. Join free and add your business details. Every member does, so you know who you're dealing with.
  2. Post what you have or what you need, or search what others have listed.
  3. Message businesses privately through DASKIES. Contact details stay private until you both choose to share them.
  4. Agree terms in a deal room. Nothing is binding until every party approves the same terms.
