One team, from your first enquiry to delivery

You deal with one DASKIES team the whole way, in your time zone. Use every step, or only the ones you need.

  1. Tell us what you need. The product or capacity, specification or drawings, quantity, target price, destination and timeline.
  2. Supplier search and checks. We shortlist Indian manufacturers and exporters that fit, check their registration and track record, and arrange video walkthroughs and site visits.
  3. Price negotiation. We negotiate price, payment terms, packing and delivery dates with the supplier on your behalf.
  4. Samples and contract. Samples are made and sent for your approval. Advocates and chartered accountants on our panel prepare and check the contract, NDA and quality terms.
  5. Order follow-up. We track production from sample approval to dispatch and send you regular updates, so problems are caught early, not at the port.
  6. Inspection before shipment. Third-party quality inspection against your agreed specification before the goods leave the factory.
  7. Export documents and customs. Invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and export customs clearance, handled through our customs broker partners.
  8. Freight and shipping. Sea or air freight booked through our freight-forwarder partners, from Mundra, Kandla, Nhava Sheva or the port that suits your order.

Four ways to work with India's manufacturers

  1. Sourcing. Any product made in India, from a single container to regular orders, bought from checked suppliers.
  2. Contract manufacturing. Parts and products made to your drawings and brand, from a pilot batch to full containers.
  3. Bulk commodities. Edible oils, groundnuts, cumin, castor oil, cotton and pulses, with grade and packing agreed in writing.
  4. Lease a running plant. Factories with machinery, power and trained staff, on monthly or long-term terms, instead of building your own.

How charges work

Register your company with DASKIES and tell us what you need. We quote for the services you choose before any work starts, per order or as a retainer for regular buying. You pay the supplier directly under your contract with them; DASKIES charges only for its own services and never holds your money.
